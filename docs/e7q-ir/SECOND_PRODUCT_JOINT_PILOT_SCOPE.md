## Reviewer task and carriers

Task: a reviewer chooses a topology-allowed `(layout, target)` pair for one declared source intent, observes which correlated candidates survive two ordered filters, and sees the exact formal coefficient and the reason for every exclusion. This is a bounded planning task. A coefficient is a signed exact rational **formal construction coefficient**, never an amplitude, probability, evidence weight, backend score or physical claim. No compiler feasibility, provider execution, circuit equivalence, optimality or speed advantage follows.

Existing [Q-A1 candidate family](CANDIDATE_FAMILY.md) `e7q.ir.candidate-family/v0alpha1` represents paired alternatives in a single factor, and `e7q.ir.candidate-restriction/v0alpha2` reports success, empty, invalid input and resource limit. Existing [Q-A2 history](TRANSFORMATION_HISTORY.md) `e7q.ir.transformation-history/v0alpha1` records ordered transforms and terminal failure. Neither schema carries a rational coefficient. The pilot must therefore propose a **separate versioned opt-in mapping record** from an admitted E7C correlated Joint row to one Q-A1 `(layout, target)` member, with a lossless signed, reduced numerator/positive-denominator pair. Do not add weights to current Q-A1 records, reinterpret member IDs, or silently conflate E7C ordering with digest-sorted E7Q member ordering.

Proposed admission for the first implementation: one known source intent; two named E7Q dimensions `layout` and `target`; finite unique Joint support; each graph-pair row maps injectively to exactly one Q-A1 member; each coefficient has nonzero signed numerator, positive reduced denominator; tags are nonempty and admitted by Q-A1. Explicitly reject duplicate/ambiguous mappings, zero coefficients, malformed fractions, unsupported graph tags and any Joint row that cannot be represented in Q-A1. Record source Joint digest and edition, row identity, original row index, member ID, exact numerator/denominator, mapping edition, and the declared interpretation of each edge predicate. A Q-A1 member ID identifies candidate bindings, not the rational coefficient; the mapping record must bind both and validate the full source Joint on replay.

Run E7C's selected two-stage **restriction of the retained correlated Joint**, with outer IR edition `/0.6` and nested first-stage IR edition `/0.5` pinned separately. In the pinned selected rule the first predicate tests absence of edge `AB` at graph coordinate 0; the second tests absence of `BC` at coordinate 1. The proposed adapter must declare and validate the limited convention `L1 ↔ AB at coordinate 0` and `T1 ↔ BC at coordinate 1` for this fixture. Stage one excludes layout `L1`; stage two excludes target `T1` among stage-one retained rows. Return complete source replay, ordered row/attempt events, charged steps, first and second exclusions, `secondStarted`, and typed terminal outcome. A failed second-attempt ledger append must charge its step and set `secondStarted = true` while appending no event or partial success. The proposed E7Q record references, but does not rewrite, the Q-A1 family and Q-A2 history identities. Return to the exact Joint source when an E7Q inventory view loses coefficients or correlation.

## Conventional comparison

Run the same admitted task under (1) the E7C Joint/source and independent IR paths; (2) a conventional **full correlated two-column candidate table** with exact fractions and explicit filters; and (3) a marginal-only Cartesian reconstruction. The full-table baseline can reproduce A and B; report its effort and audit trail honestly. The marginal-only reconstruction yields four artificial pairs with coefficient `1/4` each and cannot distinguish A from B, so it is a deliberate loss-of-correlation counterexample, not a claim that ordinary tables cannot preserve dependence. Compare accepted/rejected member sets, coefficient conservation, exclusion and event replay, non-success handling, reviewer time/errors and artifact size. Declare the sample and the independent reviewer, and do not claim a product improvement before a real workflow comparison.

## Negative and proof gates

- Reject same-marginals-as-equivalent, unknown source/member ID, altered row order or coefficient, duplicate mappings, stale Q-A1/Q-A2 digest, omitted exclusion, marginal reconstruction, unsupported graph encoding, and a Q-A2 history that records second-stage success after a first-stage terminal outcome.
- Preserve `invalid_input`, `domain_error`/`unsupported`, `undetermined`, `resource_limit`, and a valid empty residual according to each pinned evaluator's own typed domain; do not coerce them into one state. Exercise zero-step, first-attempt append failure, first-stage row exhaustion, second-attempt append failure and second-stage row exhaustion, including ledger and exact progress. Scope IR package-size budgets independently for `/0.5` and `/0.6`.
- Before a fidelity claim, show the selected E7C source rule, actual Python entry point and helper contracts, independent IR lowering and execution, serialization and actual-code-to-Lean observation relation for **all admitted inputs under stated host and resource premises**. Finite replay, AST pinning, and Lean theorems over handwritten models are evidence, not that implementation proof. Record any unproved link in the E7C completion ledger.
- Q-A3 residual-family realisation must separately show the selected mapping belongs to the residual family under declared objectives and resources. Q-A4 must separately bind source, transform order, target and observed receipt. The proposed pilot does not complete either gate or authenticate hardware evidence.
- Adoption requires exact-head semantic review, independent replay, source/assessor provenance, unchanged native IDs and APIs, and a real reviewer-task comparison against the full correlated-table baseline. Until then mark `mapped` (scope only), then at most `pilot_tested` after executable reproduction; keep product migration and release decisions separate.
