Fresh diagnostic mutations of the current 16-case good corpus distinguish two hypotheses: swapping only the ancilla bits causes eight failures (00 and 11 are swap-invariant), while reversing the full five-bit register label causes twelve failures. The reported count twelve is therefore compatible with a full-register reversal; **no count contradiction is established**. These diagnostic runs do not reconstruct the unavailable original failed run. Preserve the report and corrected successes; do not rewrite twelve to eight or erase the failure. See [fresh ordering diagnostics](WP0_VERIFICATION.md#ordering-history). **Resolution:** retain original-run provenance as unverified unless its circuit/log is supplied; reviewer checks the explicit bridge and inverse-preparation criterion. Current corrected bridge and both diagnostic hypotheses were freshly executed; historical-run identity remains unverified.
