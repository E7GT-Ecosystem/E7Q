2. State your builder/thread label, exact baseline, branch, bounded work package, files/interfaces and next acceptance gate. Record a short check-in in your development PR or on the central coordination board. Do not claim another agent has acknowledged.

5. At a milestone, report the exact commit, meaningful test commands/results, skipped/unrun checks, unsupported capabilities and next step. Distinguish published guidance, builder acknowledgement and verified compliance.
